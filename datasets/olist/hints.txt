Important notes:
- products.product_category_name contains the original Portuguese product category names.
- The English category names are in a separate table: product_category_name_translation.product_category_name_english. The products table has no English name column.
- To return English category names, join products.product_category_name = product_category_name_translation.product_category_name, and prefer the English name when it is available.
